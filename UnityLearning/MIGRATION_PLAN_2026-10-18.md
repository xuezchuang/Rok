### Phase 2: camera and framing (#1, #3, #4, #5) — done 2026-10-25
- The city opens at dxf **150** (`DefaultCameraDistance` 10000 cm = `city_default`, 20 units × 500).
- One wheel notch multiplies dxf by **10/9 out / 10/11 in** (`InputManager.cs:151`, sensitivity 0.1) with no `DeltaSeconds` term, so it is frame-rate independent.
- Zoom limits come from `MakeDefaultCameraKeys()`: min 82.4551 (`min`), max 250 (`city_bound`); no second pair of distance constants.
- The camera fills the window at a constant **7.5° vertical FOV** (`bConstrainAspectRatio=false`, `AspectRatio_MaintainYFOV`); a 2.05:1 window widens the horizontal FOV instead of adding bars.
- `-RokCameraDistance=` is a capture override: applied once at `BeginPlay`, accepted verbatim even outside the city clamp, so the 313.6 zoomed-out state stays reproducible (`E2b`, log `dxf=313.600 … clamp bypassed`). `RokCityEntryDxf` stays clamped into the city range and logs when it clamps.
- Runtime marker for acceptances: `Rok city camera initialized: distance=… dxf=… vertical_fov=… aspect=… constrain_aspect=0 axis=0`.
- Tests: `Rok.WorldMap.City.Framing`, `.WheelZoom` (live pawns at 30/120 fps), `.ZoomClamp`, `.AspectFraming` (reads the real `UCameraComponent`).
- **Residual:** at dxf 150 the object scale now matches Unity (the town hall is about the same fraction of the frame height in `C4`), but the composition differs. UE's converted city puts the buildings in the west half and leaves the east empty, so the wall falls outside the frame, while Unity fills the frame and still shows the wall. That is city layout/completeness, i.e. Phase 4, not a camera bug. The Phase 3 HUD gaps (#2 coordinate box/castle/hammer, #6 debug grid, #11 placeholder labels) are still visible in every capture.

### Phase 3: HUD correctness (#2, #6, #10, #11, nameplate) — done 2026-10-25 except #10
- **#2 city/world band.** `URokMainHudWidget::IsCityHudInsideBand` + `ApplyCityHudBand`, sampled from the pawn's dxf every tick. At or below `city_bound` (250) the coordinate box is hidden and the build hammer + world globe are shown; above it the box appears and the pair flips to the search magnifier + castle (`OpenLodUi` Position row `City=0/Tactical=1`; `MainInterfaceMediator.SetCityButton` with `limitDxf = 250`). Globe requests the world map; castle calls the new `ARokStrategyCameraPawn::FrameCityCenter()` (dxf 150 + home target). **Added 2026-10-25 (later):** the button pair also flips when the city is panned off screen while the camera is still inside the band — `IsCityButtonOutsideBand(dxf, cityInViewport)` mirrors `outSide = dxf > limitDxf || !Common.IsInViewPort2D(...)`; the coordinate box stays camera-band-only.
- **#6 grid.** `rok.DebugBuildingGrid` now defaults to 0 in every build (it was 2 in editor builds). `ARokPlayerController::ResolveBuildingGridDebugMode` forces at least the selected footprint only while a building is being placed or moved.
- **#11 placeholders.** The `本地预览` city marker is diagnostic-only (`-RokShowCityMarker`); Unity has no nameplate in the city view. The `行军队列 --/--` entry is collapsed until a march snapshot exists, which is what Unity does at 0 marches and what the tactical HUD test already expected.
- **Nameplate.** Anchored at the city card's declared visual centre instead of the actor root (the converted card sits +345/+624 cm off the root, which put the plate ~20 m away), then the source `CastleTitle` offset. Text is still the hardcoded `主城`; Unity shows `[TAG]Name` + city hall level, which needs a player-identity provider (open question 3 in the spec).
- **#10 one card per task: deferred.** Only the active task has runtime state; `RokMainHudWidget.cpp` deliberately refuses to invent side-task rows from config until task-proxy data exists (`MakeTaskRow` loop). This is a data gap, not a layout gap.

### Phase 4: city completeness (#7, #8) — investigated 2026-10-25, not implemented
- **#7 "only wall, town hall and 2–3 buildings remain at full zoom-out": no source rule found.** The evidence says Unity keeps the buildings at dxf 313.6:
  - `LevelDetailCamera.GetLodLevelByDxf` uses the serialised `m_lod_array` from `CityBuildingContainer.prefab` = `[1500, 3000, 8000, 20000, 42500]`, so 150 and 313.6 are both LOD level 0 — the same band.
  - The only dxf-driven hide in `CityGlobalMediator` is `UpdateCityWallState`: the wall and guard tower hide above `HEIGHTWALL = 1000` (`CityGlobalMediator.cs:225,1265`), i.e. not at 313.6.
  - City building prefabs (`Assets/BundleAssets/Scene/City_building/**`) carry no `LODGroup`, so nothing culls a whole building by distance.
  - `LevelDetailCastle` only scales the whole city container: `m_scale_curve` is 1 up to dxf 250, then `+0.0056/dxf`, 8 at 1500 (`CityBuildingContainer.prefab:63-…`). Nothing hides.
  - `U2` was captured between the night session and the day re-entry (`U1` 21:18, `U2` 21:19, `U1b` 21:20), so the sparse interior is most likely a reload/streaming artifact, not a rule. **Confirmation needs one settled zoomed-out Unity shot (ask the user first).** Do not implement a hide rule from `U2` alone.
- **#8 terrain outside the walls: blocked on the ground pipeline.** UE's city ground is a baked plane that fades to black; the real ground mesh/material chain is the unsolved item recorded in `UnityLearning/AGENTS.md`. Approximating it is explicitly forbidden, so this stays blocked until that pipeline lands.
- What is usable now: `U2`/`E6` still show the same *framing*; the remaining city differences are layout/completeness (fewer buildings, emptier east half), which is data (initial city state), not a camera or HUD rule.
